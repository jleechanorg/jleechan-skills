# /prw iOS evidence guide

## Outcome

Give AI RPG developers a practical, public-readable account of using the existing /prw workflow while building WorldAI's iOS client, centered on real captioned before/after evidence.

## Non-goals

No application, exported skill, review-policy, installer, merge or deployment changes. No new video capture, gameplay, paid model run, or community-library submission. Do not present mobile-web evidence as native iOS evidence.

## Acceptance

- The guide lives at docs/prw-worldai-ios-workflow.md.
- It links to the canonical workflow rather than redefining its stage rules.
- Existing share-authorized video and native stills are accessible and accurately scoped, including source identities, hashes and known comparison limitations.
- Relative links resolve against the pinned repository tree; prose and metadata exclude personal identifiers, private source and credentials.
- Documentation checks and independent review are recorded with their actual coverage; unfulfilled workflow gates stay explicit.

## User story

An AI RPG developer can see an actual captioned comparison, understand the difference between web and native proof, and apply the same evidence-oriented process to their own change.

## Proof plan

Inspect original public evidence bytes and hashes, decode the existing video, inspect representative frames and native stills, validate Markdown structure/links, and review the complete documentation diff. This proves the documentation and cited historical artifacts, not current product acceptance.

## Mock exemption

No new app interface or proposed visual design. Use existing actual captures with their original scope and clearly label the illustrative caption storyboard.

## Risk

The main risk is overstating historical or cross-platform evidence. Preserve platform/build/source boundaries, disclose unmatched state and unknown capture time, and keep private project material out of the public guide. The canonical lifecycle remains authoritative.
